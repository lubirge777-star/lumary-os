The Philosophy

We rejected the industry's speed. Every AURA fragrance rests for 12 weeks before bottling. Time is not a cost. It is an ingredient.

✦ PROCESS

The Ingredients

🌿 EXPAND

Sourced Wild

Every botanical is ethically foraged from its native region.

From the iris fields of Tuscany to the sandalwood forests of Mysore, we work directly with small collectives who have cultivated these plants for generations. No intermediaries. No compromises.

⚗️ EXPAND

Slow Maceration

12-week cold maceration preserves molecular complexity.

Heat accelerates extraction but destroys nuance. Our cold process takes three times longer but captures the full aromatic spectrum — the top, heart, and base notes in their natural proportion.

🤝 EXPAND

Master Blending

Each batch is hand-blended by a master perfumer.

Our perfumers train for over a decade before they are entrusted with a blend. Each batch is evaluated blind by three masters before receiving the AURA seal. No machine has ever approved a fragrance.
