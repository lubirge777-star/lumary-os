# Client Onboarding

## Version 1.0

---

## Overview
A structured onboarding process ensures every client project starts smoothly, expectations are aligned, and nothing is missed.

---

## Step 1: Discovery Call
**Goal:** Understand the business, goals, and requirements.

**Questions to ask:**
- What does your business do?
- Who is your target customer?
- What is the primary goal of the website? (get leads, sell products, share info, build credibility)
- Who are your top 3 competitors?
- Do you have existing branding (logo, colors, fonts)?
- Do you have photos, content, or assets ready?
- What is your budget range?
- What is your timeline?

**Output:** Project brief document.

---

## Step 2: Proposal & Agreement
**Deliverables:**
- Written proposal with scope, timeline, pricing
- 50% deposit invoice
- Simple contract/agreement covering:
  - Scope of work
  - Timeline and milestones
  - Payment terms (50% upfront, 50% on completion)
  - Revision limits (2 rounds)
  - Hosting/domain responsibilities
  - Post-launch support terms

---

## Step 3: Asset Collection
**Request from client:**
- Logo (vector preferred, or high-res PNG)
- Brand colors (or let us define based on industry)
- Photos (high-res, relevant to business)
- Written content or bullet points for each page
- Competitor websites they like/dislike
- Social media links
- Google Business Profile access (if applicable)

**Deadline:** Assets due within 5 business days or timeline shifts.

---

## Step 4: Design Phase
1. Experience Profile defined
2. Color palette selected
3. Stitch mockup generated
4. Client reviews and provides feedback
5. Up to 2 revision rounds
6. Client approves design

---

## Step 5: Development Phase
1. HTML structure
2. Tailwind styling
3. GSAP animations
4. Responsive implementation
5. Form setup
6. SEO metadata
7. Analytics installation

---

## Step 6: Review & Revisions
1. Client reviews staging URL
2. Up to 2 rounds of revisions
3. QA checklist completed
4. Final approval from client

---

## Step 7: Launch
1. Deploy to production
2. Point custom domain (if applicable)
3. Submit sitemap to Google
4. Update Google Business Profile with website URL
5. Provide client with login/access info
6. Send final invoice (remaining 50%)

---

## Step 8: Post-Launch
1. Monitor for 48 hours (check forms, analytics, uptime)
2. Send thank-you message
3. Ask for testimonial
4. Ask for referral
5. Offer maintenance plan
