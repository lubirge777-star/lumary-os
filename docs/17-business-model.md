# Business Model

## Version 1.0

---

## The Value Proposition
We do not sell websites. We sell outcomes:
- More customers (via Google visibility)
- Professional credibility (premium design)
- Time saved (we handle everything)
- Competitive advantage (most competitors have no site)

---

## Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1: Starter — 150,000 TZS
- 1-page website
- Mobile responsive
- WhatsApp button
- Contact form
- Google Maps embed
- Hosting setup guidance

### Tier 2: Business — 350,000 TZS
- Up to 5 pages
- Gallery/portfolio
- Google Maps
- Basic SEO
- Social media links
- Domain setup guidance

### Tier 3: Premium — 600,000+ TZS
- Custom design
- GSAP animations
- Booking/contact system
- 3 months support
- Priority delivery
- SEO optimization

### Add-ons
- Logo design: +100,000 TZS
- Content writing: +50,000 TZS
- Monthly maintenance: +50,000 TZS/mo
- Additional page: +50,000 TZS each

## Growth Path

### Phase 1: Solo Freelancer (Months 1-3)
- Build 10 websites
- Collect testimonials
- Refine templates
- Establish reputation

### Phase 2: Studio (Months 3-6)
- Hire 1 developer (revenue share or fixed)
- Take 15+ clients
- Build recurring maintenance revenue
- Expand services (branding, content)

### Phase 3: Agency (Months 6-12)
- 3+ team members
- 50+ total clients
- Recurring revenue covers costs
- Add AI automation, web apps

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| Underpricing to get clients | Attracts low-quality clients, unsustainable |
| Over-promising delivery times | 3 days for a premium site is fast — don't say 24 hours |
| No contract/agreement | Scope creep, unpaid work |
| Building without deposit | 50% upfront minimum |
| No portfolio to show | Hard to close without proof |
| Ignoring follow-ups | Most sales happen after 3+ touchpoints |
