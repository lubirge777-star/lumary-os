Our Story

Behind The Miangani Review

Miangani — meaning “civilization” in Swahili — is our lens for examining the ideas shaping modern Africa.

A Platform for Stories That Matter

Founded in Dar es Salaam in 2019, The Miangani Review began as a small newsletter among friends and grew into one of the most respected digital magazines covering African creativity, technology, and culture.

Our team of 36 writers, editors, and photographers spans 12 countries across the continent and its diaspora. Every story we publish is fact-checked, edited, and crafted with care because we believe the African narrative deserves nothing less than excellence.

2019

The Miangani Review launched as a weekly newsletter from Dar es Salaam with 150 subscribers.

2022

Reached 30K monthly readers and expanded to 20 contributors across 8 African countries.

2026

52K+ monthly readers, 840+ stories published, 36 writers across the continent and diaspora.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Miangani Review.

How often do you publish new stories?
We publish 3–5 new stories every week across five categories: Culture, Design, Technology, Business, and Travel. Our editorial team curates each piece to ensure depth, accuracy, and relevance.
Is The Miangani Review free?
Yes! Our weekly newsletter is completely free. We also offer a premium membership with exclusive long-form stories, ad-free reading, and early access to every edition.
Can I contribute a story?
We welcome pitches from writers across the continent and diaspora. Please submit your story idea through our contact form and our editorial team will review it within two weeks.
How can I advertise with you?
We offer sponsored content, newsletter sponsorships, and display advertising. Reach out to our partnerships team at partners@miangani.co.tz for rates and availability.
