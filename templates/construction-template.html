FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical residential project takes 3-6 months, while commercial projects may take 6-12 months. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase.
Do you provide warranties on your work?
Yes. All our work comes with a comprehensive warranty. We stand behind our craftsmanship and address any issues promptly.
What areas do you serve?
We serve the greater Dar es Salaam region and surrounding areas. For larger commercial projects, we can work anywhere in Tanzania.
