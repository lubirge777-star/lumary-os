Our Story

Creativity Meets
Craftsmanship

STUDIO NABO was born in Arusha, 2014, from a simple belief: great design has the power to transform businesses. Founded by Amara Nabo, we've grown from a solo venture into a 40+ member creative studio with offices in Arusha and Nairobi, working with brands across 15 countries.

Every project we take on is driven by purpose, precision, and a relentless commitment to quality. We don't just make things look good — we solve problems, tell stories, and build lasting impact for brands that dare to stand out.

Our philosophy is rooted in African creativity — bold, vibrant, and deeply human. We weave cultural richness into modern design, creating brand experiences that resonate globally while staying authentically local.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with STUDIO NABO.

What's your typical project timeline?
Timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Brand identity projects typically take 6-8 weeks, website builds range 8-16 weeks, and motion projects 4-8 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our Immerse phase.
How much does a project typically cost?
Every project is unique. Our pricing is project-based and tailored to your specific needs and goals. Brand identity projects start at $15K, while digital products range from $20K-$100K+. Contact us for a custom quote.
Do you work with startups and small businesses?
Absolutely. We work with businesses of all sizes, from ambitious startups to established enterprises. We offer scalable solutions and can tailor our approach to your budget and growth stage. Some of our best work has been with early-stage brands.
What's your process for getting started?
Start by filling out our contact form or booking a free discovery call. We'll learn about your project, discuss goals and budget, and if we're a good fit, we'll craft a detailed proposal and timeline for you. Response within 24 hours.
