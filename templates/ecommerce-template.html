Common Questions

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our collections, select your pieces, and add them to your cart. We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money).
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship to over 35 countries worldwide. Express shipping is free on orders over TZS 500,000. Delivery typically takes 3-7 business days.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy on all items in original condition. Bespoke pieces are final sale. Contact our concierge team to initiate a return.
