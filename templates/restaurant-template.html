Our Story

Where Passion Meets Plate

Founded by Chef Fatima in 2015, The Spice Merchant was born from a lifelong love of gastronomy. With training in Paris and experience in Michelin-starred kitchens, Chef Fatima brings world-class technique to every plate.

Our philosophy is simple: source the finest ingredients, honor their natural flavors, and present them with artistry. Every dish is a conversation between tradition and innovation.

2015

The Spice Merchant opens its doors in Dar es Salaam.

2019

Awarded "Best Fine Dining" by Tanzania Food Awards. Menu relaunch.

2026

15+ awards, 200+ 5-star reviews. A Dar es Salaam institution.
