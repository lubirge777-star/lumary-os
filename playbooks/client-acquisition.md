## Philosophy
We do not sell websites. We sell outcomes. We sell visibility. We sell credibility. We sell growth.

When you approach a business owner, you are not asking them to buy a website. You are offering them a tool that brings them more customers, saves them time, and makes them look professional.

## Finding Prospects

### Method 1: Google Maps Search
1. Open Google Maps
2. Search "[industry] in [city/area]"
3. Check each business for:
   - ❌ No website listed
   - ❌ Website is broken or outdated
   - ❌ Only Facebook page
4. Add to spreadsheet

### Method 2: Facebook Groups
- Local business groups
- SME communities
- Entrepreneurship groups
- Post: "I'm helping local businesses get online. Free website audit available."

### Method 3: Walking In
- Best conversion rate (30%+)
- Dress professionally
- Bring a tablet/laptop with demo sites
- Ask: "Have you considered getting a website for your business?"

## Cold Outreach Templates

```
Subject: Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence

Hi [Name],

I came across [Business Name] while looking for [service] in [area]. Your work looks great.

I noticed you don't have a website yet. Many of your competitors do, and I wanted to ask — is that something you've thought about?

I specialize in building premium websites for [industry] businesses in Tanzania. I can have a professional site ready in 3-5 days.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute chat to discuss?

Best,
[Your Name]
Lumary Studio
[WhatsApp link]
```

### WhatsApp (for Tanzanian businesses)
```
Hi [Name], I'm reaching out from Lumary Studio.

We build premium websites for [industry] businesses in Tanzania. I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website yet — which means customers searching on Google can't find you.

We can create a professional website with your menu/services, photos, location, and WhatsApp button — ready in 3 days.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### In-Person Script
```
"Hi, I'm [Name]. I help businesses in [area] get more customers online.

I noticed your business doesn't have a website. When someone searches for [service] on Google, they only find your competitors.

I can build you a professional website — with your photos, contact info, and WhatsApp — in just 3 days, starting from [price].

Would you like to see what that could look like?"
```

## Sales Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | "Think of it as a one-time investment that brings you customers for years. A single client from the website can pay for it 10x over." |
| "I don't have time" | "I handle everything. You just send me your photos and info. I'll have a draft ready in 3 days." |
| "Facebook is enough" | "Facebook is great, but 60% of customers search Google first. A website makes you look professional 24/7." |
| "Let me think about it" | "Of course. Can I send you a proposal and some examples? That way you can see what's possible." |
| "My nephew can do it" | "That's great! If it doesn't work out, I'm here. My websites come with animations, Google visibility, and ongoing support." |

## Referral System

After completing a project:
1. Deliver exceptional quality
2. Ask: "Do you know any other businesses that might benefit from a website?"
3. Offer: "For every referral that becomes a client, I'll give you 10% commission or a free update"
